# Remscheid scene references

Researched 9 November 2026 for the additions made that day: the two logos, the Rathaus tower, the bus, the trees and the sky. The Vaillant campus and the hotel building were already in the scene and are not re-researched here. The scene is a **compressed panorama**, not a street view: the Rathaus stands in the city centre, the Vaillant campus on Berghauser Straße and the hotel on Lenneper Straße.

## Layout decisions

- The tower takes the one open piece of sky inside the window, above the ridge between the Vaillant wing and the hotel. Nothing was moved for it.
- Both logos stay where the earlier marks stood. The hotel keeps the badge to the left of the name (the logo's own horizontal arrangement) instead of the stacked sign on the building, so the right curve of the window cannot cut it.
- Traffic now keeps to the right: cars use the far lane leftwards, the bus uses the near lane rightwards and is drawn in front of them. Its roof (`y = 134`) is 5 px below the lowest pedestrian lane.
- The viewer looks south: the sun crosses left to right, low, and clouds arrive from the west, right to left.

## Not verified

- IHG's own pages returned HTTP 403 to the research tools, so the hotel's brand rests on IHG's page title and URL as returned by search, on the builder's page and on the photograph. IHG's current corporate version of the logo was not opened. The sign is as photographed by February 2023; its state in November 2026 was not checked on site.
- No photograph showing where the logo sits on the Vaillant building was found, so the logo was only redrawn in place.
- Exact brand colour values. Vaillant green `#00917e` is a third-party figure (Brandfetch) that matches the official PNG by eye; the hotel green and the bus blue and turquoise were matched by eye to the photographs.
- The bus livery is that of the electric buses; the older diesel and hybrid fleet was not researched.
- Whether the tower clock is lit at night. The small warm light on it is an artistic assumption.
- The species of the campus trees.
